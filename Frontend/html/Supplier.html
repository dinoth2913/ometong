Why this works for everyone

A marketplace that only grows when you do

Ometong never buys or stores products, and we don't compete with you for buyers. We only earn money when a real trade happens — through supplier subscriptions and a small fee on escrow-protected orders. So more verified suppliers bring more buyers, more buyers create more matching requests, and better data makes the matching smarter for everyone.

For buyers
