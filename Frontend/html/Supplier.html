Why this works for everyone

A marketplace that only grows when you do

Omego Market doesn't hold inventory and doesn't compete with suppliers for buyers. We earn only when trade actually happens — through supplier subscriptions and a small fee on escrow-protected orders. That keeps our incentives lined up with yours: more verified suppliers attract more buyers, more buyers create more matched requests, and better data makes the AI matching sharper for everyone on the platform.

For suppliers & manufacturers

Lower customer-acquisition cost than trade shows or cold outreach, faster payment cycles via escrow, and a trust badge that shortens negotiation time with new buyers.

For Omego Market

Recurring subscription and transaction revenue, network effects that compound as both sides grow, and transaction data that keeps improving AI-matching accuracy — without ever carrying inventory risk.

For buyers

A shorter path to vetted, ready-to-ship suppliers instead of weeks of unanswered outreach and unverifiable claims.
