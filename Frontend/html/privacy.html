Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Ometong collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have — across the marketplace, messaging, and checkout.

On this page Who we are Data we collect How we use it Sharing & disclosure Payments Cookies & tracking Data retention Your rights Security Children's privacy International transfers Changes to this policy Contact us

1. Who we are

Ometong is a marketplace operated by Guangzhou Ometong International Trade Co., Ltd., connecting buyers, suppliers, and manufacturers for cross-border trade between China and markets including the USA, Canada, Europe, Sri Lanka, and India. This policy applies to everyone who uses ometong.vercel.app — buyers, suppliers, manufacturers, and visitors.

2. Data we collect

We collect information in three ways:

  • Information you give us — account details (name, email, password), business details for suppliers/manufacturers, shipping and billing addresses, order and listing content, messages sent through the chat widget, and reviews or ratings you post.
  • Information collected automatically — device and browser type, IP address, pages visited, and general usage patterns, used to keep the platform secure and improve performance.
  • Information from transactions — order history, cart and wishlist activity, and payment status (Ometong does not store full card numbers; that data is handled directly by our payment processor).

3. How we use it

We use collected data to:

  • Create and manage your account, and show you the correct dashboard for your role (buyer, supplier, manufacturer, or admin).
  • Process orders, escrow-protected payments, and shipping between you and the other party to a transaction.
  • Operate the marketplace search, category filters, and product recommendations.
  • Respond to messages sent through the chat widget or the contact form, and — where you've been told this — use anonymized conversation data to improve how that assistant responds.
  • Detect fraud, enforce our terms, and keep the platform secure.
  • Send order updates and account-related emails. Marketing emails, where sent, always include an unsubscribe option.

4. Sharing & disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only where necessary to run the platform:

  • Between transacting parties — a buyer and supplier/manufacturer see the shipping and contact details needed to complete an order.
  • Service providers — hosting (Vercel), database and authentication (Supabase), and third-party payment processors, each bound to only use your data to provide their service to us.
  • Legal reasons — if required by law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Ometong, our users, or the public.

5. Payments

Checkout on Ometong is designed to work with third-party payment processors capable of handling cross-border settlement between China and buyer markets such as the USA, Canada, Europe, Sri Lanka, and India. Card and bank details are entered directly with that processor — Ometong's servers never see or store your full card number.

6. Cookies & tracking

We use essential cookies and browser storage (such as localStorage) to keep you logged in, remember your cart and wishlist between visits, and remember basic preferences like theme. We do not use these to build advertising profiles.

7. Data retention

We keep account and order data for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward to meet legal, accounting, and dispute-resolution obligations. You can request deletion of your account data at any time — see "Your rights" below.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below — we'll respond within a reasonable timeframe.

9. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards — including encrypted connections, access-controlled databases, and row-level security policies — to protect your data. No system is 100% secure, but we work to keep the platform safe and to respond quickly if something goes wrong.

10. Children's privacy

Ometong is intended for business use and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.

11. International transfers

Because Ometong connects buyers and suppliers across China, the USA, Canada, Europe, Sri Lanka, and India, your data may be processed in a country other than your own. Wherever it's processed, we require the same standard of protection described in this policy.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the platform evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of Ometong after a change means you accept the updated policy.

13. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach us at hello@ometong.com or through the Contact Us page.
