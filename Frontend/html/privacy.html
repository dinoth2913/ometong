Privacy Policy

Last updated: Aug 2026 — This page explains how Ometong collects, uses, and protects your information.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly (account details, listings, orders, messages), information about transactions (orders, payments), and technical data (IP address, device, cookies).

How we use information

We use data to provide and improve the service, process orders, communicate with you, detect fraud, and comply with legal obligations. We may use aggregated or anonymized data for analytics.

Payments

We use third-party payment processors to handle payments. Payment information is processed by the payment provider and is not stored on our servers except for transaction references required to reconcile orders. See the provider's terms for details.

Cookies & local storage

We use cookies and localStorage for session management, cart persistence, preferences (language, theme) and analytics. You can clear or block cookies through your browser settings, but some features may stop working.

Sharing & disclosure

We share data with service providers necessary to operate the service (hosting, email, payments), with your consent, or when required by law. Suppliers may see order data necessary to fulfill orders.

Your rights

You may access, correct, or delete your personal data by contacting us at hello@ometong.com. We will respond according to applicable law.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect data. No internet service is completely secure; if you suspect a breach, contact us immediately.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hello@ometong.com or use the Contact page.
