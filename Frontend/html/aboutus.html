✦ Who we are

One trading company, built to make global sourcing simple

Ometong connects verified suppliers, manufacturers and buyers worldwide — with direct RFQ quoting, escrow-secured payments and integrated logistics, all in one place.

Our Story

Sourcing from China, shouldn't mean juggling five different middlemen

Ometong is operated by Guangzhou Ometong International Trade Co., Ltd. — we source and export products and services, and handle the logistics chain ourselves, door to door, to the United States, Canada, Europe, Sri Lanka and India.

Instead of buyers chasing suppliers through cold emails and suppliers chasing payment through unfamiliar buyers, Ometong puts verification, secure payment and logistics on one platform — so both sides can focus on the actual deal, not the paperwork around it.

Verified suppliers only

Every supplier is document-checked and rated before they can list.

Escrow-secured payments

Funds release only when delivery is confirmed on both sides.

Logistics built in

Freight, customs and delivery arranged without leaving the platform.
